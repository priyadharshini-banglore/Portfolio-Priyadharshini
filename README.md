> *A conversational, hands-free voice assistant engineered for logistics and delivery drivers on the road.*

- 🗺 **Dynamic GPS & Navigation**: Drivers ask 'what is the next stop?' to calculate live route coordinates and render interactive navigation maps.
- 🛠 **Tech Stack**: `n8n Workflows` `Gemini Voice AI` `Firebase Firestore` `React.js` `Live Maps & Routing`

<br/>

#### 🍲 [Online Food Waste Tracker (MERN Stack + AI Analytics)](https://github.com/priyadharshini-banglore)
> *An AI-powered restaurant management ecosystem that reduces food waste through customer ordering patterns and live inventory intelligence.*
- ⚡ **AI Smart Menus**: Recommends items dynamically based on consumer purchasing habits and perishable stock expiration.
- 📊 **Real-time Inventory Analytics**: Live kitchen dashboard reducing food surplus by up to 35%.
- 🛠 **Tech Stack**: `React.js` `Node.js` `Express.js` `MongoDB` `AI Analytics`
